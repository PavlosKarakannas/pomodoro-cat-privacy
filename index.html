Privacy Policy for Pomodoro Cat

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Pomodoro Cat ("the app") is a Pomodoro study timer developed by Pavlos Karakannas. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can control it.

Summary

By default, the app collects nothing at all and makes no network connections. Two optional features involve data, and both require your explicit action to turn on.

Analytics (off by default, opt-in)

If you turn on Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data, the app collects:

  • A random installation ID generated on your device. It is not linked to your name, email, account, advertising ID, or any other identifier.
  • Which screens you open (Timer, Stats, Wardrobe, Settings).
  • Whether study sessions are completed or skipped.
  • Whether a purchase was started, completed, or failed (only which product, never payment details).
  • Your app version, platform (Android/iOS/web), and the time of each event.

We never collect your name, email, precise location, device model, advertising ID, contacts, or any text you type into the app.

This data is sent to a database we control (hosted on Supabase) and is not sold or shared with any third party, and is not used for advertising.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy. Turning it off immediately deletes your installation ID and any data queued but not yet sent.

Purchases

The app offers optional one-time cosmetic purchases (extra cats and outfits), handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not process, see, or store your payment details — that is handled by Google under Google Play's own privacy policy. We only learn which product ID you bought, so we can unlock it in the app.

Data retention and deletion

  • If you never opt in to analytics, we hold no data about you at all.
  • If you opt in, your data is retained until you either opt out (which deletes your installation ID and any undelivered data) or contact us to request deletion (see below).

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
